How to read these signals

MATCHES shows the most frequent digit in the selected historical window. DIFFERS shows the least frequent digit. Signal strength is the observed historical percentage, not a probability forecast. Past digit frequency does not guarantee the next tick; synthetic digits may be independent.

V9 prioritizes synthetic indices and skips markets that return a market-closed response. It automatically selects a market once live ticks arrive. This tool is manual-only. It does not request account authorization and cannot place, buy, sell, or manage trades. Confirm the market and contract in Deriv yourself.
